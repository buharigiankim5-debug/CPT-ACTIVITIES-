TAHO RECIPE

Taho is a popular Filipino snack made with soft tofu, sweet arnibal (brown sugar syrup), and tapioca pearls (sago).

Ingredients

For the Tofu

  • 4 cups unsweetened soy milk
  • 1 teaspoon gypsum powder (calcium sulfate) OR 2 tablespoons unflavored gelatin powder (dissolved in 1 tablespoon warm water)

For the Arnibal (Syrup)

  • 1 cup brown sugar or muscovado sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

For the Pearls

  • 1/2 cup small tapioca pearls (sago)
  • Water (for boiling)

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Cook the Tapioca Pearls

  1. Bring a pot of water to a rapid boil.
  2. Add the tapioca pearls.
  3. Lower the heat and cook for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Drain the pearls, rinse with cold water, and set aside.

Step 2: Make the Arnibal (Brown Sugar Syrup)

  1. Combine the brown sugar and water in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle boil over medium heat.
  3. Stir until the sugar dissolves completely.
  4. Simmer for about 5 minutes until the syrup thickens.
  5. Add vanilla extract if desired.
  6. Turn off the heat and set aside.

Step 3: Prepare the Tofu

  1. Bring the soy milk to a gentle simmer.
  2. Place the dissolved gelatin or gypsum powder in a heat-proof container.
  3. Pour the hot soy milk into the container without stirring.
  4. Cover and let it set for 15–20 minutes until firm and jiggly.

Step 4: Assemble and Serve

  1. Scoop the warm tofu into a glass or mug.
  2. Pour the arnibal over the tofu.
  3. Top with cooked tapioca pearls.
  4. Mix gently and enjoy while warm!

Tips

  • Serve the taho while it is warm.
  • Adjust the sweetness by adding more or less arnibal.
  • Use fresh tofu for the best texture and flavor.
